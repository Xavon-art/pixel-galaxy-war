Pixel-War
---------
 just a simple survival game where you pilot a green pixel ship against waves of red invaders. I added a 3-life system to make it a bit more forgiving, and a scrolling starfield background to give it that 'infinite space' depth. It’s fully responsive, so you can play with WASD on your desktop or use the on-screen D-pad if you’re on your phone.
